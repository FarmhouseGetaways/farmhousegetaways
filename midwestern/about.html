About

A little bar built on a Wisconsin memory

The Midwestern was a private bar, pub and game room modeled after the classic country bars of the Midwestern United States — most of all, rural Wisconsin.

The room

Pub & arcade games, free to play

Pub and arcade games were plentiful and free to play. The bar had professional air hockey, three arcade machines, an 800-watt professional karaoke system, an ATV figure-8 dirt track, a shooting range, and darts. UFC events showed there, and NFL RedZone ran all season long.

The Midwestern was all about the ’80s. Journey on Pandora was the staple, but you were guaranteed a soundtrack that ranged from today’s hip-hop to ’80s metal, depending who was picking.

Quick facts

The short version

What it was
A private, Wisconsin-style bar and game room — not a public storefront, but built and run like one.
Where
Oceanside, California.
Games
Professional air hockey, three arcade cabinets, electronic darts, an 800-watt karaoke rig, an ATV figure-8 track, and a shooting range.
On screen
NFL RedZone all season, UFC on fight nights.
On the walls
A collection of vintage, lit-up beer signs — Old Style, Miller Lite, Schmidt’s, and more.
The soundtrack
Journey, mostly, on Pandora — with detours into hip-hop and ’80s metal.
The regular event
Friday night air hockey, 8pm, USAA-sanctioned ratings matches — see the Air Hockey page.
Darts

A little money always rode on it

The electronic board saw as much action as the air hockey table — usually with a stack of ones on the line and someone lining up a shot for the room.
