Air Hockey

Professional air hockey at The Midwestern

A beautiful 1990s vintage Tornado table, kept in serious condition — one of three known left in existence.

The table

Fast, smooth, and rare

The table was a genuine 1990s Tornado, kept in excellent condition — one of three known left in existence. Most of the top pros who played on it commented on how fast and smooth the surface still was. A variety of pucks and mallets stayed on hand, for every kind of player, beginner to serious competitor.

Every Friday, 8pm

Join our monthly air hockey league!

Come learn and play competitive air hockey on the best professional table in Southern California. Serious new players were always welcome, with real instruction for anyone wanting to learn the game — every Friday night, starting at 8pm.

Organized, USAA-sanctioned ratings matches ran every Friday at The Midwestern, hosted through proairhockey.com. Players were ranked and updated within 24 hours on airhockeyworld.com. Everyone was welcome to join.

Inquiries used to go to airhockey@themidwestern.com — kept here for the record; the bar is closed, so treat it as archival rather than a working contact.

General info

The rest of the room

The table sat at the center of a room that also held three arcade cabinets, an electronic dartboard, and a bar lined with stools — everything free to play, all night, whether or not you were there for the league.
