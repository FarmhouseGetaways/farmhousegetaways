What was there

A whole game room, free to play

Everything in the room got used. Nothing was coin-operated.

Professional air hockey

A 1990s Tornado table — one of three known left in existence — kept fast and smooth, with a rotation of pucks and mallets for every kind of player.

Arcade & darts

Three arcade cabinets and an electronic dartboard, all free to play, all night.

800-watt karaoke

A full professional karaoke system, ready whenever someone worked up the nerve.

ATV track & range

A figure-8 dirt track and a shooting range rounded out the outdoor side of things.

Bar food & a heated patio

Wings and bar food, NFL RedZone and UFC on the screen, and a heated patio for when the room filled up.

Vintage beer signs

Old Style, Miller Lite, Schmidt’s — a wall of lit-up, stained-glass beer signage collected over the years.

Every Friday, 8pm

Friday night was air hockey night

Organized, USAA-sanctioned ratings matches ran every Friday, hosted through proairhockey.com, with players ranked and updated within 24 hours on airhockeyworld.com. Everyone was welcome to join — new players got real instruction, not just a table to lose on.

The bar has closed

The Midwestern is no longer open. This page was built from what remains of its original site, to keep a record of the room, the games, and the Friday nights, for anyone who was there.

It was also written up, in its time, on Facebook and Yelp — those pages are still up, if you want to see how others remembered it.
