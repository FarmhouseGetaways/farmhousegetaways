Right now — the last 30 minutes

Every other number on this page is hours old. Google Analytics processes its main reports on a delay, so a click you just made will not appear below for several hours — that is normal, not a fault. This panel comes from Google Analytics' separate realtime feed and updates within seconds, which makes it the only honest way to answer "did that click actually register?". To test the tracking: open a property page, press Book Now, then reload this page — it should appear here immediately.

The booking funnel

Four steps, in the order a guest actually moves through them. Each number is people doing the thing; the percentage under it is how many of the people at the previous step made it this far. Where the percentage collapses is where you are losing bookings.

Where the traffic comes from

Platforms

The actual site or app someone was on immediately before landing here. Google Analytics stores these as raw hostnames — m.facebook.com, l.instagram.com, (direct) — so they are renamed here to what they really are. Direct means no referrer at all: typed the address, a bookmark, or a link from a text message or email app.

Channels

Google's own grouping of those platforms into marketing categories. Organic Search is free Google/Bing results. Direct is people who already knew you. Referral is another website linking to you. Paid Search and Paid Social are ads — both should be zero until a campaign launches, so anything appearing there before then is worth a look.

Do the other brands send bookings?

Mini Barn Market and Farmstand.TV are separate Google Analytics properties, so their traffic cannot be added to this one. What can be answered here is the question that actually matters: of the people who became a lead or started booking on this site, how many arrived from the other two brands. That is whether the cross-linking earns its keep.
