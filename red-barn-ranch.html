2,200 square feet of it

The barn is where the kids go and do not come back.

Pool table, shuffleboard, arcade basketball, a pinewood derby track, a putting green cut into the floor, and a wall of cabinets on free play. It is heated, it is enormous, and it buys the adults about four hours.

The stay

Built for the group that never gets to be in one place.

Most houses that sleep twenty are just big. This one is arranged so that a family of fifteen with kids from six months to seven years old can occupy it without anyone losing their mind. The bedrooms are spread out. The kitchen is stocked like someone actually cooks in it. The barn absorbs every child on the property from about four in the afternoon.

Outside there are four acres to run around: a greenhouse, a vineyard, a bird garden, and the animals. In the morning we will take whoever is awake out to gather eggs.

The Industrial Mini Mansion — a tiny home on the property — is the seventh bedroom. Out of season it rents on its own for two.

On the property, not in town

The farm stand is a ninety-second walk from the back door.

Mini Barn Market sits at the back entrance of the ranch and is open every hour of every day on the honor system. Guests wander down in pajamas for eggs and sourdough before anyone else is up.

It is the highest-rated thing we run, and it is fifty steps from where you are sleeping.
