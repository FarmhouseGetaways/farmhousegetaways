Your hosts

We live on the ranch, and we love having people here.

Cory and Carissa. Hosting is the part of this we love most — and whatever your family wants from the week, we are glad to be here for it. An early morning at the coop with whoever is awake. A quiet stretch with nobody around you at all. A hand at eleven at night when the pool light will not turn off.

A lot of stays begin with a code and end without you ever meeting anyone. That works perfectly well for a city apartment. A farm is a little different — there are animals to meet, a garden to walk through, and questions that are simply easier to answer in person.

So we do it the other way around. We live here on the property, and we think of your stay as something we are hosting rather than something we are renting. When a family with seven children under seven stayed with us last year, we walked the whole crowd out to the coop in the morning and taught them which breed lays the blue eggs. That is the part of this we love most.

We are also very happy to leave you be. Some groups want to be shown everything, and some would rather have the place entirely to themselves for a week. Both are lovely, and you only have to tell us which.

What we also do

We make the show about farm stands.

After we built Mini Barn Market, it became obvious that Ramona had a farm stand culture nobody outside the county knew about. So we started Farmstand.TV, a series about the hidden places across Southern California where you can buy food from the people who grew it.

Then we drew the Ramona Farmstand Map — thirty-plus active stands, plus the wineries and the trails and everything else worth stopping for. It is the reason people now drive out here specifically to eat their way across a town most of San Diego has never visited.
