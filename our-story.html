Your hosts

We live on the ranch. That is the whole difference.

Cory and Carissa. We are the people who will take your kids to gather eggs, call the neighbor to get you onto a farm that is closed to the public, and be here at eleven at night when the pool light will not turn off.

Most short-term rentals are run by somebody you never meet. A code arrives, you let yourself in, and if something goes wrong you send a message into a void. That is a fine way to rent an apartment. It is a terrible way to spend a weekend on a farm.

We built this the other way around. We live here, on the property, and we treat the stay as something we are hosting rather than something we are renting. When a family with seven kids under seven showed up last year, we walked the whole crowd out to the coop in the morning and taught them which breed lays the blue eggs. Then we called two neighbouring farms and got them in to pick fruit.

None of that is a package. It is just what happens when the hosts are standing right there.

What we also do

We make the show about farm stands.

After we built Mini Barn Market, it became obvious that Ramona had a farm stand culture nobody outside the county knew about. So we started Farmstand.TV, a series about the hidden places across Southern California where you can buy food from the people who grew it.

Then we drew the Ramona Farmstand Map — seventeen active stands, plus the wineries and the trails and everything else worth stopping for. It is the reason people now drive out here specifically to eat their way across a town most of San Diego has never visited.

What we are trying to do

Show people that this is easy.

  • OrganicThe property is farmed without synthetic inputs, and the food in the stand comes from us or from people we know by name.
  • SustainableNot as a marketing word. As the reason the greenhouse, the birds and the vineyard are all on four acres together.
  • Hands onGuests collect eggs, walk the vineyard, meet the animals. Kids especially.
  • HonestThe farm stand runs unattended on the honor system, and it works. That says something about the people who come out here.

Come and stay, and we will show you around properly.
