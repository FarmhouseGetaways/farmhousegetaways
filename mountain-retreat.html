Built like a pub, plays like an arcade.

A proper counter, booth seating, a lounge, and eighties arcade art across the walls. Seven arcade machines, a 75-inch television with an Xbox, air hockey, foosball, side-by-side basketball and darts.

It runs all day for the children and stays open all evening for everyone else.

The house

One floor, one big room, and a deck to spill out onto.

Two thousand square feet of house on eight acres, with everybody able to gather in one room and still spill out onto the deck. The kitchen has a copper sink and a gas range and opens straight onto the living space, so whoever is cooking is not cut off from everyone else.

Every bedroom is on the ground floor. There are two steps up at each entrance to the house, so it is level once you are inside but not entirely step-free.

Fourteen sleep here comfortably as a mix of grown-ups and children. Six of the beds are proper beds and two are fold-down couches, and the bunk room suits children far better than it suits adults — so we would say ten adults is the happy maximum, with children filling out the rest.

The land

The trails start at the back door.

You do not drive to a trailhead here. You walk off the deck, across the footbridge, and you are on it — single track through manzanita and scrub oak, granite boulders the size of cars, boardwalk where the ground gets wet in spring. A seasonal stream runs through the property and there is a small bridge waterfall when the water is up.

Iron Mountain runs off the back by way of our own trails. Potato Chip Rock, at the top of Mt. Woodson, is a mile or two away for anyone who wants the real climb before breakfast.

We are far enough from town that the stars come out in full nearly every night. Most groups end up at the fire pit or in the hot tub, staying out much later than they meant to.

The question everybody actually asks

What do we do all day if we never leave?

The trails begin at the back fence, so the walk starts before anyone has decided to take one. There are eight acres of hiking trails and three holes of disc golf for whoever needs to burn something off. There is a horseshoe pit for the people who would rather stay near the deck.

The arcade runs through the afternoon. Dos Picos Park and its fishing pond are a short walk away, which is the closest thing to an outing you will need.

In the evening it is the fire pit and the hot tub, with the stars out overhead.

The practical part

Good internet, and a house that runs itself.

There is Wi-Fi at the main house and in the game room, and it holds up with a full house on it. Three smart TVs — one in the game room, one in the living room, and one in a back bedroom.

Every entrance is on a smart lock and keypad, so there is no key to collect on the way in. The air conditioning is connected, and the lighting and irrigation are automated. There is no EV charger here.

The hot tub is on year round and ready whenever you are, day or night. It sits under a locked cover rather than behind a gate — worth knowing if you are bringing very small children.

One less thing to pack

Set up for families with young children.

Two pack-n-plays, high chairs and toys. Every bedroom is on the ground floor. Three or four families fit here comfortably, which is the size a lot of groups are actually looking for, and there are eight acres for everyone to spread out into.

The bathrooms and the laundry room are stocked too — shampoo, conditioner, hand and body soap, and laundry soap. They are ours, they are chemical-free, and they are what we use ourselves. Bring your own if there is something you are loyal to; plenty of people do. But they are there, and we hope you like them.

The place to yourselves

The whole property is yours.

Mountain Retreat is private. For the length of your stay the house, the arcade, the hot tub, the fire pit and all eight acres belong to your group and nobody else — no shared drive, no other booking alongside you, nobody passing through.

We are a few minutes down the road and always reachable. Call or text whenever you need anything at all, however small, and we will take care of it. The rest of the week is yours.

Before you book

Good to know

This is a peaceful rural property, best suited to guests who appreciate nature, privacy and a slower pace. The terrain is natural and uneven in places, there is wildlife, and conditions change with the season. That is most of the appeal, but it is worth knowing before you arrive.
