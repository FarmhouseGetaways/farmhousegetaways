The stay

Not a farm. A mountain.

Two thousand square feet of house, plus a seven-hundred square foot pub-style arcade — a proper counter, a lounge, a booth to sit in, and eighties arcade art across the walls. It is built like a bar and plays like an arcade, which means the grown-ups have somewhere to be once the kids have gone down. Outside: a fire pit, a hot tub, horseshoes, a disc golf run, and private trails that climb straight off the property.

Iron Mountain trailhead is minutes away and Potato Chip Rock is the next ridge over, so this is the one to book if somebody in the group wants to hike before breakfast. Dos Picos Park is walkable. So are six wineries, which is the other reason people book it.
