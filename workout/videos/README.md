# Videos

Drop an exercise video in here and put `videos/name.mp4` in the editor's Video
field. It then plays inside the app, and works with no signal once the phone
has seen it.

Keep them small. A phone in a garage is on a poor connection and every megabyte
is hers to download — a few hundred kilobytes of a ten-second loop is plenty to
show a movement. Anything much over 10 MB belongs on YouTube as an unlisted
video, with the link pasted into the same field, rather than in this repository.
