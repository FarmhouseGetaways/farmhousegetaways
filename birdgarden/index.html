At Red Barn Ranch

A garden built for the birds, with room for the whole family to visit.

Our bird garden is about three thousand square feet, fully enclosed and solar-powered, and it is home to exotic chickens, ducks, turkeys and peacocks.

The birds are our pets, and they earn their keep with the eggs we sell at Mini Barn Market. We tend them most days, so there is usually someone close by who is glad to show you around.

How the garden works

The garden takes care of itself, so we can take care of the birds.

The whole garden is enclosed and runs on solar power. The duck pond drains and refills on its own, and the coop windows and doors open and close with the weather.

The ranch’s Internet reaches the bird garden too, in case someone would like to send a photo home from the coop.

Your hosts

We tend the birds most days, and we are always glad of company.

We are close by, and you will probably spot us coming and going from the bird garden. The animals need tending whether there are guests here or not, which means the birds come with someone glad to show you around.

How much you see of us is up to you. Some groups would rather have the place to themselves for the week, and we stay out of the way. Others would love a bird tour, or to meet us at the Chicken Garden and gather eggs together. Just tell us when you arrive.
