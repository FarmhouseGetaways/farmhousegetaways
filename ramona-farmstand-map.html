The Ramona Farmstand Map

More than thirty working farm stands sit on this plateau. Most of them are at the end of a road you would never think to turn down, a good number run on the honor system, and almost none of them appeared on any map. So we drove the roads and gave them a home.

Tap any pin for the address, the hours and what they sell. Everything on here has been visited in person.

The part that is not on the map

Which ones are worth stopping for. That we will only tell you by email.

A map tells you where the stands are. It does not tell you which one has the good sourdough, which one restocks on Thursday morning, which two are worth the drive out past the wineries and which one you can skip.

We live here and we film these places for a living, so we know. Give us an email address and we will send you the part we cannot put on a map.

  • The print-quality illustrated map, for the glovebox.
  • Our half-day loop — the exact route we send our own guests on.
  • What is ripe right now, and which stands restock on which days.
  • Word when good weekends open up, before they reach the listing sites.

Where the map came from

We make a show about farm stands.

Carissa hosts Farmstand.TV, which is exactly what it sounds like: a show about the farm stands of Southern California. Mapping Ramona was the first thing we did, because it was the town we knew and nobody had done it.
