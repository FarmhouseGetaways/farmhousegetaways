Why here

The venue is also the hotel, the backdrop, and the morning after.

Most venues rent you six hours. This is a ranch, so you get the whole weekend: the rehearsal dinner under the trees, the ceremony outside, the reception in a 2,200 square foot barn, and breakfast the next morning with eggs from the birds thirty feet away.

We live here, which means you are not coordinating with a rental company. You are talking to the two people who will be on the property the entire time.
