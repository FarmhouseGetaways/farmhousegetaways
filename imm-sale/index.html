The house

A small house, made with a big house in mind.

The Industrial Mini Mansion is a custom-built tiny home of 258 square feet. It is one bright, open space with a queen bed, a kitchenette, a bathroom with a full-size shower, and a seating lounge. The 11' ceilings make it feel spacious.

Outside it is corrugated steel, laid in a patchwork of panels, with black-framed windows and a pair of upcycled leaded-glass French doors. Inside it is warm: white shiplap walls, wood floors, recessed lights and a WIFI enabled smart digital fireplace beneath an Alexa enabled Fire TV.

It has been one of our guest rooms, and we have loved it. Now it is ready for its next home.
