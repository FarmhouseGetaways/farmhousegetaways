We are not a wedding venue. We are where the people you love most wake up that weekend.

Why a house instead of a hotel block

A venue rents you six hours. The weekend is the part people remember.

Hotel blocks scatter everyone across three floors and a lobby. Here the whole group is under one roof, on four acres, with a kitchen big enough to actually cook in and a barn the children happily disappear into for the entire afternoon.

The morning of, there is room for everyone to get ready without queueing for a mirror. The morning after, there is a farm stand ninety seconds from the back door and enough coffee for everybody.

Most groups book two or three nights. Some take both properties and put thirty-four people within a few miles of the venue.
