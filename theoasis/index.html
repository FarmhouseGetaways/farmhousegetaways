Red Barn Ranch

The part of the ranch where everybody ends up, sooner or later.

The pool and spa sit together in one fully gated area we call the Oasis, with wine grape covered fencing, and mature King Palms shading the gathering spots around it.

It is where the morning swim happens, where the afternoon stretches out, and where the evening finishes in warm water. Some of the family will be in the pool, and some in the shade, and that is exactly how it should be.

For families with little ones

The water sits inside its own fence.

The pool and the spa are inside one fully gated area, set apart from the rest of the four acres. For a family travelling with little ones, that is one of the first things worth knowing.

The house is ready for small guests too: three pack-n-plays, high chairs and a good supply of toys, and the main house has no stairs.

Warm water

Open year round, and warm when you want it.

It stays open year round and warms up on its own through the summer. Outside those months it can be heated upon request — ask us for seasonal rates, and give us about five days’ notice for heating in cold months. Two hours of heated spa are included every day, at no charge.
