At the back gate of Red Barn Ranch

Mini Barn Market never closes, and nobody works there.

Walk out the back of the ranch and there is a farm store, open every hour of every day, run entirely on the honor system. You write down what you took, you total it up, you pay by card or Apple Pay or cash in the box.

Eggs from the birds you met yesterday. Produce, mushrooms, sourdough, soap from people who make it a few miles from here. It is the most-reviewed thing we have, and every review says some version of the same thing: they could not believe it was real.

Not nearby. Here, on the ranch, which is why guests keep writing our names into their reviews. We take the kids out to gather eggs and tell them which bird laid which one. We call the neighbors and get you onto farms that are not open to the public.

And then there is the rest of it

Ramona is the part nobody expects.

  • Wine countryMore than thirty wineries within ten miles. Several close enough to Red Barn Ranch to walk.
  • Farm standsSeventeen active stands on the map we drew, most of them run on the same honor system as ours.
  • Iron MountainTrailhead minutes from Mountain Retreat. Potato Chip Rock is the next ridge over.
  • JulianApple pie, gold mines, and pine forest, an hour up the road.
  • Safari ParkTen miles. Twenty-five minutes, and you are looking at rhinos.
  • San DiegoThirty-five minutes to downtown, which is the part guests never believe until they drive it.
