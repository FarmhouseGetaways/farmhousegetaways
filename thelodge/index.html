From the family behind Mini Barn Market

Our first farm stand, and our next.

Mini Barn Market is our first farm stand. It sits at the back gate of Red Barn Ranch, open twenty-four hours a day on the honor system, with eggs, sourdough, produce, mushrooms and the soaps we make on its shelves.

The Lodge will be our next, a short drive across town at Mountain Retreat. What it will carry is still to come, and we will share it here once it is.

Where it is going

Pine, granite and Iron Mountain.

The Lodge is coming to Mountain Retreat: eight acres of pine and granite at the base of Iron Mountain, at an elevation of 1,545 feet. The trails start at the back door, and the stars come out in full nearly every night.

Guests keep telling us it feels like Colorado, then remembering they are half an hour from San Diego.
