The part nobody expects

Thirty-five minutes from San Diego, and nothing like it.

Ramona has more than thirty wineries, twenty-odd farm stands, one of the best-known climbs in the county, and almost no traffic. We drew the map, so this is the honest version of what is worth your time.

Start here

The Ramona Farmstand Map

Seventeen active stands, most of them unattended and running on the honor system exactly like ours. Some are a folding table with a cash tin. Some are a whole barn. We built the map because there was no way to find them otherwise.

It is the best single day you can have here: drive the loop, buy something at each stop, end up at a winery.

Wine country

Thirty-plus wineries, and you can walk to some of them.

  • From the ranchSeveral wineries within walking distance of Red Barn Ranch. This is the reason wedding parties book here.
  • From the retreatSix within three miles of Mountain Retreat.
  • The wider valleyMore than thirty within ten miles, most of them small and family-run.
Outside

Trails, rocks and one very famous photograph

  • Iron MountainTrailhead minutes from Mountain Retreat. Roughly six miles round trip, and the view at the top covers the whole valley.
  • Potato Chip RockUp Mt. Woodson. The queue for the photo is real on weekends. Go early.
  • Dos Picos ParkOak groves, a pond, and picnic tables. Walkable from Mountain Retreat.
  • Mt. Woodson Golf ClubEighteen holes threaded through boulders.
Day trips

Worth leaving the property for

  • Safari ParkTen miles, twenty-five minutes. The tram is the point.
  • JulianAbout an hour into the pines. Apple pie, gold mines, and twenty degrees cooler in August.
  • Downtown San DiegoThirty-five minutes. Guests never believe this until they drive it.
  • The coastAbout an hour to the beaches, which means you can do mountains and ocean in the same day.
