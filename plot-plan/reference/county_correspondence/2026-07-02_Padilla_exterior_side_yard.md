## What it establishes, and what it does not

**Establishes (use this):** the corner where the store sits is an **Exterior Side
Yard**. That designation was the only genuinely discretionary call on the west
side — front yard versus exterior side yard — and it is now in writing from PDS
and was independently confirmed by her supervisor at the counter in September.

**Does not establish:** the number. She quoted half the rule. PDS-306 **page 5**
(not page 3) states it in full:

> "An exterior side yard setback must be 35 feet measured from the centerline of
> the abutting street **or must equal the interior side yard setback, whichever is
> greater.**"

On this parcel Whirlwind's centreline is 30' west of the west property line, so:

| Half of the rule | Where the setback line lands |
|---|---|
| 35' from the centreline | 5' inside the west P.L. |
| Equal to the 15' interior side yard, from the lot line | 15' inside the west P.L. |
| **Whichever is greater** | **15' from the west P.L.** |

So the supervisor's 15' is the ordinance-correct answer, not a concession, and
the sheet's 15' line has a published citation behind it. Do not "restore" the 35'
from this email — it is the lesser setback and would be wrong.
